plumber journeyman 8 aac 90.130 description:
a plumber journeyman certificate of fitness authorizes the holder of the certificate to perform work that is subject to the standards established in as 18.60.705 and 8 aac 63.010.
application:
application packet must include:
application, fees, experience verification form(s).
experience:
while holding a trainee cof (in alaska):
8,000 hours of legally obtained work in installation of commercial/residential plumbing.
a maximum of 4,000 hours of military/vocational experience per 8 aac 90.890(b).
a maximum of 1,000 hours of classroom training.
a maximum of 500 hours of utility plumbing work.
a maximum of 2,000 hour of gas work.
no maintenance hours will be accepted.
work performed in alaska without a valid plumber trainee cof will not be counted.
equivalent work in other states must be worked legally in that state.
other:
for out-of-state applicants:
applicant must submit one of:
license issued by the origin state or letterhead letter from the state confirming that no license is required for the scope of work.
test:
exam required.
passing score 70%.
if the applicant fails the examination, the applicant must wait 30 days before reexamination.
fees:
$50 non-refundable application fee.
a separate $200 certificate fee upon issue.
duration:
2 years renewal:
application , $200 certificate fee.
renewal within two years of expiration.
continuing education of 16 hours within the preceding 24-month period.
at least eight hours must cover significant changes or updates to the uniform plumbing code, and no more than eight hours may be industry related.

plumber restricted utility 8 aac 90.137 description:
a plumber restricted pu certificate of fitness authorizes the holder of the certificate to install:
(1) water service piping from the street, right-of-way main, water well, or storage tank to the first stop valve of the serviced structure or other point of use or distribution on the serviced property;
(2) building sewer piping from within two feet of a serviced structure to a public sewer, private sewer, private sewage disposal system, or other point of disposal;
and (3) building storm drains that receive the discharge of roof drains, leaders, downspouts, or conductors, if the storm drains are installed from within two feet of a commercial structure, or from within two feet of a residential structure of four or more dwelling units, to the street, the right-of-way main line, or a point of disposal approved by the federal, state, or local agency having jurisdiction.
application:
application packet must include:
application, fees, experience verification form(s).

1,000 hours of work in installation of water, sewer, or storm lines.

a maximum of 125 trade-related classroom hours.
other:
for out-of-state applicants:
applicant must submit one of:
license issued by the origin state or letterhead letter from the state confirming that no license is required for the scope of work.
test:
exam required.
passing score 70%.
if the applicant fails the examination, the applicant must wait 30 days before reexamination.
fees:
$50 non-refundable application fee.
a separate $200 certificate fee upon issue.
duration:
2 years renewal:
application , $200 certificate fee.
renewal within two years of expiration.

plumber utility trainee 8 aac 90.145 description:
a trainee certificate of fitness authorizes the holder to perform work subject to the standards established in as 18.60.705 and 8 aac 90.137(a) performed under the conditions set out under this section.
if the trainee's job requires the trainee to perform work subject to the standards of as 18.60.705 and beyond the limits of the plumber utility specialist, the trainee must be licensed subject to the requirements of 8 aac 90.140.
the trainee will be under the direct supervision of a journeyman plumber or a restricted plumber pu certificate of fitness holder at all times while performing to the code;
and the trainee's activities will be limited exclusively to the functions outlined in 8 aac 90.137 while performing the functions of a plumber utility specialist.
the ratio of individuals working under trainee certificates on a job site may not be more than two plumber utility trainees for every certified plumber journeyman.
application:
application packet must include:
application, fees, notarized letter from employer.
experience:
none.
other:
submit a notarized letter from the applicant's employer on company letterhead stating that the trainee will be under the direct supervision of a journeyman plumber or a restricted plumber pu certificate of fitness holder at all times while performing to the code;
and trainee's activities will be limited exclusively to the functions outlined in 8 aac 90.137 while performing the functions of a plumber utility specialist.
test:
no test required.
fees:
$50 nonrefundable application fee plus $200 certificate fee.
duration:
6 years renewal:
nonrenewable.
may re-apply.

explosive handler 8 aac 62 description:
in connection with an excavation, tunnel, quarry, earth removal, or construction carried on in this state, a person may not be employed without a certificate of fitness, while engaged in emplacing explosives for detonation;
installing primers, fuses, wires, or other means of detonation;
or detonating explosives.
application:
application packet must include:
application, verification of experience, fees, course completion certificate, and either a completed fingerprint card or current federal clearance letter.
experience:
at least six months of documented, hands-on experience in this state as a chuck tender, driller, or helper of a holder of a certificate of fitness.
other:
criminal background requirements in 8 aac 62.020-030.
applicant may instead submit the applicant's original documentation issued by the united states bureau of alcohol, tobacco, firearms and explosives (usatfe) showing the applicant's successful background check conducted within the 18 months preceding the date of application by usatfe for the purposes of obtaining a federal license for the transportation, shipping, receiving, or possession of explosive materials.
this is either the federal clearance letter showing applicant's name in the responsible person section, or confirmation of a successful background check.
requires completion of 16-hour department-approved course no more than 90 days prior to application.
test:
department examination required.
passing score 75%.
if the applicant fails the examination, the applicant must wait 30 days before reexamination.
maximum two examinations in any 6-month period.
fees:
$150 + $47 background check fee, if needed.
bg fee is $50 as of october 1, 2026.
duration:
3 years renewal:
as above, but only 8-hour refresher course required.
